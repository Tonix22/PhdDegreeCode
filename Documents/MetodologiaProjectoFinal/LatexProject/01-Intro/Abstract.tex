\section{Abstract}

Project proposes a framework for data recovery in wireless communication systems, focusing on dynamic environments characterized by Doppler effects and delay-induced distortions. The solution integrates a mixture of experts by selecting the strengths of neural networks and traditional approaches, the framework is designed to intelligently manage high-mobility scenarios such as vehicle-to-vehicle communication and Industry 4.0 applications. A classifier-based intelligent administrator, which dynamically optimizes the trade-off between time, computational resources, and performance, ensuring efficient handling of signal distortions under varying conditions.

This address challenges of real-time communication in high-mobility scenarios by providing a scalable and adaptive solution that significantly improves performance while meeting computational and time constraints.

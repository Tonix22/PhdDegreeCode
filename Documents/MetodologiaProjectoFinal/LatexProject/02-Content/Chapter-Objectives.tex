\section{Objectives}
\subsection{General Objective}

Develop an efficient data recovery framework for high-mobility 5G wireless communication channels affected by Doppler and delay effects, using a mixture of experts approach to enhance performance in Industry 4.0 applications

\subsection{Specific Objectives}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Classify channel impairments caused by Doppler and delay effects using advanced neural network techniques.
    \item Develop and evaluate neural network models (e.g., LSTM, CNN, Vision Transformers) that clean Doppler-shifted signals and mitigate distortions.
    \item Optimize the trade-off between computational resources, performance, and time for real-time deployment in high-mobility environments.
    \item Test the proposed framework in controlled simulated scenarios to evaluate its performance under realistic channel and hardware constraints.
    \item Deploy and validate the framework on embedded devices for integration in Industry 4.0 applications, such as autonomous vehicles and industrial robotics.
\end{enumerate}

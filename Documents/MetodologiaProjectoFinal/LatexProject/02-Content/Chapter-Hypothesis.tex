\section{Hypothesis}

\subsection{Investigation}
The Doppler effect and delay in high-mobility wireless communication channels generate distortions in the signal-to-noise ratio and inter-signal interference, negatively affecting the recovery of transmitted data through 5G and OFDM networks.

\subsection{Null}
The Doppler effect and delay in high-mobility wireless communication channels do NOT generate significant distortions in the signal-to-noise ratio or inter-signal interference.

\subsection{Alternative}
The distortions in the signal-to-noise ratio and inter-signal interference in high-mobility channels are NOT primarily caused by the Doppler effect and delay but rather by other factors such as thermal noise, external interference, or modulation errors.

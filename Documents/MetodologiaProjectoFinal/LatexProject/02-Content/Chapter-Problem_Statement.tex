\section{Problem Statement}

High-speed mobility environments, such as those involving industrial robots, drones, or autonomous vehicles, present significant challenges for telemetry in Industry 4.0. These systems rely on real-time data transmission for critical operations, but communication in such scenarios often involves a high volume of data packets, which may require retransmission when data is not received correctly. Poor perfomance in system equalizers struggle to recover the transmitted information effectively where a bad recived message or failure in communication could result in the complete loss of equipment or, even worse, pose serious risks to personnel safety.

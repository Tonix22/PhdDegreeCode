\section{state of art}

One of the most relevant stages for a neural network (NN) is preprocessing. As seen in the article, values are multiplied by the channel transpose to transform distorted values into a closer cluster. With this, an NN can learn better because the points are closer, and the calculated weights should not be too big. Normally, large weights can lead to difficulties in training because of the gradients.

Secondly, there is new research in NNs with transformers that can map and interact with relationships between current data and past data. This makes it more robust because, in the double dispersive logic that involves time and frequency, it can learn more complex patterns over the signal transmission and equalize the signal to become cleaner.
